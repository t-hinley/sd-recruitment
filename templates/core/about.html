Who we are

A recruitment partner for employers and candidates

SD Recruitment is a recruitment partner for employers and candidates. We work with businesses that need reliable, capable staff, and with people who are looking for work or planning their next career move.

For employersWe manage the time-consuming parts of hiring: understanding the role, finding and screening candidates, and coordinating interviews through to placement. Hiring with us For candidatesWe offer more than job matching. Our career support services, including resume support, interview preparation and career counselling, help people present their experience well and approach opportunities with confidence.
